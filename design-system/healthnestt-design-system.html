Healthnestt Design System

The shared visual language for the public site, freelancer dashboard and admin dashboard. Tokens are CSS variables so Tailwind and any component code can map to them directly.

1. Principles

Health readers are often worried and on a phone. The design should feel calm and credible, and never look like an ad.

CalmLots of white space, one brand color, no visual noise.
CredibleAuthor, dates, sources and disclaimer always visible on articles.
ReadableSerif body at 19px+, high contrast, short line length.
Mobile firstDesign at 360px wide, then scale up. 44px touch targets.

2. Color

Teal reads as health and trust without the clinical blue of most medical sites. A warm amber accent is reserved for tips and disclaimers. Neutrals have a slight cool tint.

11. Accessibility and quality rules

RuleStandard
ContrastWCAG AA: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI borders
Touch targetsAt least 44 by 44 px
FocusVisible 3px focus ring on every interactive element, never removed
Color aloneNever the only signal: statuses and errors always include text
ImagesAlt text required on every cover and inline image
HeadingsOne H1 per page, headings in order
MotionRespect reduced-motion, keep transitions under 200ms
Languagelang="en" now, per-page lang when Hindi is added
PerformanceImages in modern formats and lazy-loaded, fonts limited to two families

Content tone

  • Plain language at about a grade 8 reading level, explain any medical term once.
  • Never diagnose or give dosages. Use "may", "can" and "talk to a doctor".
  • Every article ends with sources and shows the disclaimer near the top.
